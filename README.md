# RVIalternativeNDVI 
The study was conducted using Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data sets collected from March 1, 2020, to March 31, 2021, in the Musi watershed of Telangana, India. The study found that RVI could be used instead of NDVI for vegetation monitoring, particularly during the monsoon season, when cloud cover can be a significant issue. The time-series analysis of NDVI and RVI showed that RVI provide expected results similar to NDVI during monsoon season of India. Unlike NDVI, which becomes saturated after a flowering stage of cotton, the RVI increased linearly as the cotton-boll grows. Specifically, RVI continued to increase up to November, whereas NDVI became saturated mostly in the second week of October 2020. The study suggests that RVI could be a useful alternative to NDVI for Cotton monitoring, particularly in areas where cloud cover is a problem.
